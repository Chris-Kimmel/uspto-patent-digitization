VERTICAL BLIND
Paul Cureton, Riverside, Calif.
(450 Springs Road, Vallejo, Calif.)
Filed Sept. 30, 1955, Ser. No. 537,674
8 Claims. (Ci. 160-118)
The present invention relates to vertical slat blinds,
and its primary object is to provide a new and improved
blind wherein the slats are drawn between open and
closed positions, and also tilted through substantially
180° about their longitudinal axes, by a single control
cord.
